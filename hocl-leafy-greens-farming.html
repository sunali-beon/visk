Common Threats

Why Leafy Greens Are the Highest Food Safety Risk

Leafy greens are eaten raw — and most contamination travels through water, soil, and handling surfaces.

E. coli / Salmonella

Escherichia coli O157:H7 / Salmonella spp.

Leafy greens cause 48% of foodborne outbreaks. Contamination from irrigation water, soil, handling, and animal intrusion. Consumers eat greens raw — no kill step.

VISK Solution: 30–50 ppm produce wash achieves 7.85-log reduction of E. coli (vs. 4.87-log for chlorine).

Downy Mildew

Bremia lactucae / Peronospora spp.

Yellow patches on upper leaf surface, white-grey fuzzy growth underneath. Rapid spread in cool, humid conditions. Makes greens unmarketable.

VISK Solution: 50 ppm foliar spray every 5–7 days. Begin preventive sprays at first sign of cool, humid weather.

Pythium (Damping Off / Root Rot)

Pythium spp.

Seedlings collapse at soil line (damping off). In hydroponics, roots turn brown and slimy. Devastating in recirculating systems.

VISK Solution: 2–5 ppm continuous in hydroponic nutrient solution. 50 ppm soil drench in soil-based systems.

Soft Rot Bacteria

Pectobacterium / Erwinia

Slimy, water-soaked decay of leaves and heads. Foul smell. Spreads rapidly in packed containers during transport and storage.

VISK Solution: 50 ppm foliar spray. 30 ppm post-harvest wash. Sanitize storage containers with 200 ppm.

Application Protocol

Stage-by-Stage VISK Protocol for Leafy Greens

1

Seed Treatment

When: Before sowing

  • Soak seeds in 100 ppm VISK for 20 minutes. Especially critical for organic production where chemical seed treatments aren't used.
  • Key Risk: Seed-borne bacteria, Pythium

Dilution: 100 ppm · 20 mL/100L · Soak 20 min

2

Germination / Propagation

When: Day 0–10

  • Mist germination trays with 50 ppm every 2 days. Sanitize trays/plugs with 200 ppm before seeding.
  • Key Risk: Damping off, Pythium, Rhizoctonia

Dilution: 50 ppm · 10 mL/100L · Mist

3

Seedling Growth

When: Day 10–25

  • 50 ppm mist every 3 days. In hydroponics: add 2–5 ppm to nutrient solution from day one.
  • Key Risk: Root rot, early downy mildew

Dilution: 50 ppm mist · 2–5 ppm hydro

4

Transplant / System Entry

When: Day 20–30

  • Soil: 50 ppm root dip + 100 ppm bed drench. Hydro: ensure 2–5 ppm maintained in solution.
  • Key Risk: Transplant-borne pathogens, root zone colonization

Dilution: 50 ppm dip · 2–5 ppm solution

5

Vegetative Growth

When: Day 25–50

  • 50 ppm foliar spray every 5–7 days. 2–5 ppm continuous via drip or in hydro solution.
  • Key Risk: Downy mildew, bacterial leaf spot, E. coli from water

Dilution: 50 ppm · 10 mL/100L · Weekly spray

6

Pre-Harvest

When: 3–5 days before cutting

  • 30 ppm foliar spray. Ensure full leaf coverage. This is your last protective spray.
  • Key Risk: Surface bacterial contamination, downy mildew

Dilution: 30 ppm · 6 mL/100L · Full coverage

7

Harvest Wash

When: At cutting

  • Immediately submerge cut greens in 30–50 ppm VISK solution for 2–5 minutes. Gentle agitation.
  • Key Risk: E. coli, Salmonella, Listeria — the critical food safety moment

Dilution: 30–50 ppm · 6–10 mL/100L · Dip 2–5 min

8

Post-Harvest / Packing

When: Within 1 hour of harvest

  • Air dry on sanitized racks. Pack in sanitized containers. Cold chain immediately — 2–4°C.
  • Key Risk: Bacterial multiplication at warm temperatures, soft rot

Dilution: 200 ppm container sanitize · Cold chain
