Key Challenges

Why Aquaculture Needs HOCl

RAS Water Treatment

Recirculating Aquaculture Systems

Pathogens recirculate with water through the entire system. Traditional disinfectants harm biofilters and fish.

VISK Solution: 0.5–2 ppm continuous in system water. Safe for biofilters at this dose. ORP target: 200–400 mV.

Pond Preparation

Pre-stocking and between cycles

Pond bottoms harbor Vibrio, EHP (Enterocytozoon hepatopenaei), and other pathogens from previous cycles.

VISK Solution: After draining and drying, spray pond bottom and walls with 200 ppm. Fill with treated water — dose 2–5 ppm before stocking.

Hatchery Sanitation

Broodstock, larvae, and nursery tanks

Larvae and fry are extremely vulnerable to Vibrio, Aeromonas, and fungal infections. Biosecurity is critical.

VISK Solution: Sanitize all tanks, nets, and equipment with 200 ppm between batches. Add 0.5–1 ppm to larval rearing water. Dip broodstock in 5 ppm for 30 seconds before spawning.

Equipment Sanitation

Nets, feeders, aerators, pipes, boats, vehicles

Equipment moved between ponds or tanks can spread disease across the entire farm.

VISK Solution: Spray/dip all equipment in 200 ppm before moving between ponds. Foot baths with 200 ppm at pond entrances.

Live Transport

Harvest and transport of live fish/shrimp

Stress during transport reduces immunity. Crowded transport tanks are ideal for disease spread.

VISK Solution: Add 1–2 ppm to transport water. Sanitize transport tanks with 200 ppm before loading.

Application Protocol

Stage-by-Stage VISK Protocol for Aquaculture

1

RAS Water Treatment

When: Continuous

  • Add 0.5–2 ppm to system water. Safe for biofilters at this dose. ORP target: 200–400 mV.
  • Key Risk: Pathogen recirculation in closed systems

Dilution: 0.5–2 ppm · 0.1–0.5 mL/100L · Continuous

2

Pond Preparation

When: Pre-stocking and between cycles

  • Spray pond bottom and walls with 200 ppm after draining and drying. Fill with treated water — dose 2–5 ppm before stocking.
  • Key Risk: Vibrio, EHP from previous cycles

Dilution: 200 ppm surface · 2–5 ppm fill water

3

Hatchery Sanitation

When: Between batches and during rearing

  • Sanitize all tanks, nets, and equipment with 200 ppm between batches. Add 0.5–1 ppm to larval rearing water. Dip broodstock in 5 ppm for 30 seconds before spawning.
  • Key Risk: Vibrio, Aeromonas, fungal infections in larvae

Dilution: 200 ppm equipment · 0.5–1 ppm water · 5 ppm dip

4

Equipment & Infrastructure

When: Before transfer between units

  • Spray/dip all equipment in 200 ppm before moving between ponds. Foot baths with 200 ppm at pond entrances.
  • Key Risk: Cross-contamination between ponds and tanks

Dilution: 200 ppm · 40 mL/100L · Spray/dip before transfer

5

Live Transport

When: Harvest and transport

  • Add 1–2 ppm to transport water. Sanitize transport tanks with 200 ppm before loading.
  • Key Risk: Stress + crowding = ideal conditions for disease spread

Dilution: 1–2 ppm transport · 200 ppm tank sanitize
