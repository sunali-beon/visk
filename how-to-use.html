From irrigation lines to harvest wash — practical protocols, dilution ratios, and step-by-step guides for every farm type.

Application Methods

Six Ways to Apply VISK on Your Farm.

Choose the method that fits your farm setup. VISK Kavach works across all standard farm equipment — no specialised devices needed.

Drip Irrigation Dosing

Add VISK to your irrigation water line through a Venturi injector or dosing pump. Maintains clean drip emitters and eliminates biofilm buildup in water lines.

2–10 PPM in water line

Foliar Spray / Misting

Spray diluted VISK on plant surfaces using a knapsack sprayer or misting system. Ideal for leaf surface hygiene in polyhouses and open-field crops.

50–100 PPM foliar solution

Dip / Soak

Submerge seeds, cuttings, tools, or harvested produce in a VISK solution for contact-time sanitation. Works with standard tubs, trays, and wash tanks.

50–200 PPM depending on item

Surface Wipe

Spray or wipe VISK solution on equipment, trays, crates, floors, cold rooms, and pack-house surfaces. For daily sanitation between harvests or shifts.

100–200 PPM surface spray

Reservoir / Tank Dosing

Dose VISK directly into water tanks, nutrient reservoirs, or mixing tanks. Essential for hydroponics, aquaponics, and controlled environment farms.

2–5 PPM in reservoir

Fogging / Fumigation

Atomise VISK solution into fine mist for ambient air sanitation inside polyhouses, cold rooms, and pack-house environments. Covers all exposed surfaces.

100–200 PPM fogging solution

Hydroponics & Root Zone

Root Rejuvenation with VISK.

Recirculating hydroponic systems are breeding grounds for biofilm, root-zone pathogens, and algae. VISK Kavach keeps your root zone healthy without harming plants or beneficial microbes at recommended doses.

The Root Zone Problem

⚠

Biofilm Buildup

Bacteria colonise pipe walls, channels, and root surfaces — forming a sticky biofilm layer that blocks nutrient uptake and harbours pathogens. Iron bacteria in well water can cause a 44× increase in bacterial counts from wellhead to emitter.

⚠

Root Browning & Slime

Pythium, Fusarium, and other water-borne organisms attack root tips in warm, oxygen-depleted zones. Roots turn brown, slimy, and lose the white, fibrous structure needed for healthy nutrient absorption.

⚠

Algae & Clogging

Algae growth in reservoirs and channels competes for dissolved oxygen, clogs drippers and nozzles, and creates organic matter that feeds pathogen populations. Warm water and light exposure accelerate the problem.

VISK Root Zone Protocol

A

Preventive Dosing

When: Every reservoir change or continuously in recirculating systems.

  • Maintain 2–3 PPM free available chlorine in the nutrient reservoir.
  • Add VISK concentrate to the reservoir after mixing nutrients and adjusting pH.
  • Use an ORP meter — target 650–750 mV for optimal sanitation without plant stress.
  • Top up dosing as VISK degrades (typically every 24–48 hours depending on organic load).

Result: Clean root zone, clear water lines, zero biofilm buildup. Roots stay white and fibrous.

B

Root Recovery Treatment

When: Roots are already brown, slimy, or showing signs of rot. Nutrient solution smells off.

  • Drain the existing nutrient solution completely.
  • Fill the system with fresh water dosed at 5–10 PPM VISK.
  • Run the system (recirculate) for 30–60 minutes — this flushes the root zone and breaks down biofilm.
  • Drain, refill with fresh nutrient solution, and return to preventive dosing (2–3 PPM).

Result: Visible improvement within 48–72 hours. New white root tips emerge once the pathogen load is reduced.

C

System Flush (Between Crops)

When: Between growing cycles. After removing plants and before replanting.

  • Remove all plant material from the system.
  • Fill with VISK solution at 50–100 PPM.
  • Run for 2–4 hours to sanitize channels, pipes, pumps, and reservoirs.
  • Drain completely. Rinse once with clean water.
  • Refill with fresh nutrient solution — ready for the next crop.

Result: Complete sanitation of the entire system. Eliminates residual biofilm, algae, and pathogens from the previous crop cycle.

System-Specific Notes

NFT (Nutrient Film Technique)

Thin film channels clog fast. Use preventive dosing (2–3 PPM continuous) and flush channels weekly at 50 PPM. Monitor the return-flow end for biofilm buildup.

DWC (Deep Water Culture)

Roots sit in standing water — the highest risk for root rot. Maintain 2–3 PPM at all times. Keep dissolved oxygen above 6 mg/L alongside VISK for best results.

Dutch Buckets / Ebb-Flow

Return lines and drain buckets accumulate organic debris. Flush return lines monthly at 50 PPM. Add VISK to the main reservoir for continuous protection.

Aquaponics

Use lower concentrations (1–2 PPM) to protect beneficial bacteria in the biofilter. Dose after the biofilter, not before it. Monitor fish behaviour when adjusting concentration.

Post-Harvest

Produce Wash & Food Safety.

VISK Kavach is food-grade and leaves no residue. Use it as a produce wash to extend shelf life and meet food safety standards — from farm gate to retailer.

01

Prepare the wash solution

Fill your wash tank with clean water. Add VISK Kavach concentrate to achieve 50–100 PPM free available chlorine. Stir or circulate to mix evenly. Verify with a chlorine test strip.

02

Dip the produce

Submerge freshly harvested produce for 1–3 minutes. For leafy greens and berries, 1 minute is sufficient. For tomatoes, capsicum, and root vegetables, allow 2–3 minutes of contact time.

03

Drain and pack

Remove produce and allow to air dry or use a produce dryer. No rinse required — VISK breaks down to saline and leaves no harmful residue. Pack immediately for transport or cold storage.

Monitor your wash water: As organic matter enters the wash tank, the effective chlorine concentration drops. Check PPM every 30 minutes during heavy use and top up VISK as needed. Replace the entire wash solution after every 500 kg of produce or when turbidity is visible.

Pro Tips

Get the Most from VISK.

Do's

  • Store VISK in a cool, dark place — HOCl degrades in heat and UV light.
  • Always dilute before use — never apply concentrate directly.
  • Use clean water for dilution — high organic load reduces efficacy.
  • Add VISK after mixing nutrients and adjusting pH in hydroponic systems.
  • Monitor with test strips or an ORP meter for consistent results.
  • Flush water lines gradually — sudden high doses can dislodge biofilm and cause sludge.
  • Keep the product sealed between uses.
  • Test on a small area first when applying to a new crop variety.

Don'ts

  • Don't mix VISK with other chemicals — especially acids, bleach, or hydrogen peroxide.
  • Don't use expired product — check the manufacturing date and shelf life.
  • Don't store in direct sunlight or above 35°C.
  • Don't exceed recommended PPM — higher isn't better. Excess can stress sensitive crops.
  • Don't increase dosage suddenly when algae appear — biofilm must dissolve gradually to prevent sludge.
  • Don't re-use heavily contaminated wash water — replace it.
