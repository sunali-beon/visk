12 Pathogens Every Farmer Should Know

Three Categories of Farm Pathogens

Understanding pathogens is the first step to eliminating them. This guide covers the 12 most damaging farm pathogens — their biology, symptoms, and how VISK neutralizes them.

Fungi — The Most Common Threat

Botrytis cinerea (Grey Mold), Fusarium oxysporum (Fusarium Wilt), Powdery Mildew (various species), Anthracnose (Colletotrichum spp.). Reproduce via spores, survive in soil and debris, spread by wind, water, and tools.

VISK Solution: 50 ppm foliar spray destroys spore viability. 100 ppm soil drench targets soil-dwelling stages.

Oomycetes — Water Molds

Pythium (Damping Off / Root Rot), Phytophthora (Blight / Crown Rot), Peronospora (Downy Mildew). Thrive in water, spread through irrigation systems, attack roots and foliage, survive in soil for years.

VISK Solution: 2–5 ppm in irrigation water for continuous control. 100 ppm drench for active outbreaks.

Bacteria — Fast, Invisible, Devastating

E. coli O157:H7, Salmonella, Xanthomonas (Bacterial Spot/Blight), Ralstonia (Bacterial Wilt), Pectobacterium (Soft Rot). Invisible to naked eye, multiply rapidly in warm/moist conditions, spread through water and contact.

VISK Solution: 50 ppm spray for plant bacteria. 30–50 ppm wash for food safety (7.85-log E. coli reduction).

Application Protocol

VISK Treatment Protocols by Pathogen Type

1

Fungal Pathogens

When: Prevention + Cure

  • Preventive: 50 ppm foliar spray every 5–7 days.
  • Curative: 100 ppm spray at first sign of symptoms, repeat every 3 days for 2 weeks.
  • Soil Treatment: 100 ppm drench at planting for soil-borne species.
  • Environmental: 50 ppm fogging in greenhouses to reduce airborne spore load.
  • Key: Prevention is always more effective and cheaper than cure.

Dilution: 50 ppm preventive · 100 ppm curative · Fogging optional

2

Oomycete Pathogens

When: Continuous water treatment

  • Preventive: 2–5 ppm continuous in irrigation water.
  • Curative: 100 ppm soil drench around affected plants. In hydroponics: increase to 10 ppm for 24 hours.
  • System Maintenance: 100 ppm flush of irrigation system between crop cycles.
  • Key: These pathogens live in water — treat the water first.

Dilution: 2–5 ppm continuous · 100 ppm drench · 100 ppm flush

3

Bacterial Pathogens

When: Continuous + food safety

  • Preventive: 50 ppm foliar spray. 2–5 ppm in irrigation water.
  • Curative: 100 ppm spray on affected areas. Remove severely affected plants.
  • Food Safety: 30–50 ppm produce wash for 2–5 minutes (critical for leafy greens).
  • Biosecurity: 200 ppm tool sanitation between plants/rows to prevent spread.
  • Key: Bacteria spread through contact — sanitation is the only effective tool.

Dilution: 50 ppm spray · 30–50 ppm wash · 200 ppm tools

4

Viral Pathogens

When: Indirect control

  • Note: VISK does not directly kill plant viruses (viruses require living cells to exist). However, VISK controls the vectors and bacterial co-infections that worsen viral damage.
  • Vector Control: Sanitize tools with 200 ppm to prevent mechanical transmission (TMV, PepMV).
  • Stress Reduction: Healthy, pathogen-free plants are more resistant to viral damage.
  • Key: Prevent virus spread through hygiene, not treatment.

Dilution: 200 ppm tool sanitation · Indirect protection
