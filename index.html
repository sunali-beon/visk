What's Inside

Simple. Natural. Effective.

VISK is formulated from just three ingredients: purified water, salt, and a gentle electrolysis process. The result is a naturally oxidised solution that's pH-balanced at 5.5 — matching your skin's own pH.

After use, VISK simply breaks down into salt and water, leaving no residue and no ecological footprint. It's personal care that's kind to you and to the planet.
