2.1 Product Classification

VISK products are farm sanitation and hygiene inputs based on Hypochlorous Acid (HOCl). They are intended for use in water treatment, surface sanitation, equipment cleaning, and post-harvest produce washing in agricultural and allied operations.

2.2 What VISK Products Are Not

VISK products are not pesticides, fungicides, insecticides, bactericides, or crop-protection products.
