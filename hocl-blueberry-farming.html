Common Threats

The Diseases That Destroy Premium Blueberry Value

Blueberries face relentless fungal pressure — from bloom through cold storage.

Botrytis Grey Mold

Botrytis cinerea

Grey fuzzy mold on ripe and harvested berries. The #1 post-harvest loss in blueberries. Infection often occurs at bloom but symptoms appear at harvest.

VISK Solution: 30 ppm bloom spray every 3–5 days. 50 ppm post-harvest dip. Cold chain below 2°C immediately.

Anthracnose Fruit Rot

Colletotrichum acutatum

Sunken lesions with orange spore masses on ripe fruit. Spreads explosively in warm, wet conditions. Major issue in southern growing regions.

VISK Solution: 50 ppm foliar spray every 7 days from green fruit. Increase frequency during rainy periods.

Mummy Berry

Monilinia vaccinii-corymbosi

Fruit shrivels and mummifies on the bush. Fungus overwinters in fallen mummy berries on the ground. Infects through flowers.

VISK Solution: 100 ppm ground spray at bud break to suppress apothecia. 50 ppm bloom spray.

Phytophthora Root Rot

Phytophthora cinnamomi

Wilting, yellowing, plant decline. Roots turn brown and die. Worse in poorly drained soils. Can kill mature bushes.

VISK Solution: 50 ppm root zone drench every 2 weeks. Improve drainage. 100 ppm drench at first sign of symptoms.

Application Protocol

Stage-by-Stage VISK Protocol for Blueberry Farming

1

Dormant / Pre-Season

When: Before bud break

  • Sanitize pruning tools with 200 ppm between bushes. 100 ppm ground spray under bushes to suppress mummy berry apothecia.
  • Key Risk: Mummy berry inoculum, canker diseases

Dilution: 200 ppm tools · 100 ppm ground spray

2

Bud Break / Green Tip

When: First green tissue visible

  • 50 ppm foliar spray as buds open. Continue ground sprays for mummy berry.
  • Key Risk: Mummy berry shoot strikes, Phomopsis

Dilution: 50 ppm · 10 mL/100L · At bud break

3

Pink Bud

When: Flower buds showing color

  • 50 ppm foliar spray covering buds and foliage. This is the critical mummy berry infection window.
  • Key Risk: Mummy berry flower infection

Dilution: 50 ppm · 10 mL/100L · Every 5 days

4

Bloom

When: During flowering

  • 30 ppm fine mist every 3–5 days. Avoid disrupting pollinators — spray early AM or late PM.
  • Key Risk: Botrytis bloom blight, mummy berry

Dilution: 30 ppm · 6 mL/100L · Fine mist

5

Green Fruit

When: Petal fall to fruit sizing

  • 50 ppm foliar spray every 7 days. Begin anthracnose prevention program.
  • Key Risk: Anthracnose latent infection, Botrytis

Dilution: 50 ppm · 10 mL/100L · Weekly

6

Ripening / Color

When: First color to harvest

  • 30 ppm foliar spray every 5 days. Minimize fruit wetting — use fine mist.
  • Key Risk: Anthracnose, Botrytis, ripe rot

Dilution: 30 ppm · 6 mL/100L · Every 5 days

7

Harvest

When: At picking

  • Sanitize harvest containers with 200 ppm. Handle gently — bruised fruit develops Botrytis faster.
  • Key Risk: Mechanical damage + Botrytis, field heat

Dilution: 200 ppm container sanitize

8

Post-Harvest

When: Within 1 hour of picking

  • 50 ppm produce wash dip for 2–3 minutes. Air dry. Force-air cool to 0–2°C immediately.
  • Key Risk: Botrytis, anthracnose, sour rot

Dilution: 50 ppm · 10 mL/100L · Dip 2–3 min
