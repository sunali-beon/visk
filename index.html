The science, simply put

What is VISK?

VISK Kavach is hypochlorous acid (HOCl), the same molecule your own immune system produces to fight infection. It is manufactured with electrolyzed water technology, using only salt, water and electricity.

HOCl helps eliminate biofilm, bacteria, fungi and viruses. Under recommended use conditions it leaves no chemical residue, and after use it breaks down into salt water.
