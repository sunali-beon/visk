The Post-Harvest Problem

3 Contamination Points You Can’t Ignore

Fresh produce travels from field to fork through multiple contamination points — harvest, handling, washing, packing, transport, and retail. Each step adds risk.

Surface Pathogens

E. coli O157:H7, Salmonella, Listeria monocytogenes — present on produce surfaces from soil, water, handling, and animal intrusion. Leafy greens cause 48% of produce-linked outbreaks. One contaminated batch can trigger national recalls.

VISK Solution: 30–50 ppm produce wash for 2–5 minutes achieves 7.85-log reduction (vs. 4.87-log for chlorine).

Cross-Contamination

Pathogens spreading from one batch to another through shared wash water, handling equipment, and storage surfaces. One contaminated item can infect an entire wash batch. ‘Wash’ becomes ‘contamination event’ without proper water treatment.

VISK Solution: VISK maintains kill power in wash water throughout the shift. Unlike chlorine, efficacy doesn’t drop as organic load increases.

Post-Harvest Decay

Botrytis grey mold, anthracnose, soft rot bacteria — pathogens that activate after harvest, especially in cold storage. Up to 40% of fresh produce is lost post-harvest in developing countries.

VISK Solution: 30 ppm produce wash extends shelf life by 3–7 days by eliminating surface decay organisms.

Application Protocol

5-Step VISK Post-Harvest Protocol

1

Setup

When: Before first batch

  • Fill wash tank with clean water.
  • Add VISK to achieve 30–50 ppm (6–10 mL per 100L).
  • Verify with ORP meter (target >650 mV) or test strips.
  • Use potable water. Pre-filter if using well or canal water.

Dilution: 30–50 ppm · 6–10 mL/100L

2

Prepare Produce

When: Before washing

  • Remove field soil and debris.
  • Trim damaged portions.
  • Sort — remove any produce with visible rot or severe damage.
  • Pre-cooling helps if produce arrives warm.

Key: Sort & trim before washing

3

Wash

When: 2–5 minutes contact time

  • Submerge produce in 30–50 ppm VISK solution for 2–5 minutes.
  • Gentle agitation improves contact.
  • Do not overcrowd the tank.
  • VISK maintains efficacy even as organic load increases — unlike chlorine.

Dilution: 30–50 ppm · 2–5 min submersion

4

Dry

When: Until surface dry

  • Air dry on sanitized stainless steel or food-grade racks.
  • Use fans if available.
  • Do not stack wet produce.
  • Moisture promotes bacterial growth during storage — thorough drying is critical.

Key: Air dry on sanitized racks

5

Pack & Cold Chain

When: Within 1 hour of washing

  • Pack in sanitized containers (spray with 200 ppm before use).
  • Enter cold chain within 1 hour.
  • Target: 2–4°C for most produce.
  • Break in cold chain negates most post-harvest treatment benefits.

Dilution: 200 ppm container spray · 2–4°C

Pack-House Hygiene

Keep Your Cold Room Clean Between Loads

Cold storage rooms are breeding grounds for Botrytis, Penicillium, and Listeria — pathogens that survive and even grow at refrigeration temperatures.

1

Surface Spray

When: Between loads

  • Spray cold room walls, floor, and racks with 200 ppm VISK solution.
  • Allow 10 minutes contact time before loading new produce.

Dilution: 200 ppm · 40 mL/100L

2

Cold Room Fogging

When: Before loading new produce

  • Fog cold room with 100 ppm VISK for 30 minutes.
  • Eliminates airborne spores and surface pathogens in hard-to-reach areas.

Dilution: 100 ppm · 30 min fogging

3

Humidifier Treatment

When: Continuous

  • Add 2 ppm VISK to cold room humidifier water.
  • Prevents microbial growth in humidification system and on stored produce.

Dilution: 2 ppm · Continuous

4

Packing Surface Sanitation

When: Start and end of each shift

  • Sanitize all packing surfaces, conveyor belts, and equipment with 200 ppm.
  • Include sorting tables, scales, and packaging tools.

Dilution: 200 ppm · 40 mL/100L
