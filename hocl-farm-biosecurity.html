Common Threats

3 Ways Disease Enters Your Farm

Every farm — whether greenhouse, open field, poultry, or aquaculture — has the same three entry points for disease.

People & Visitors

Farm workers, contractors, agronomists, delivery drivers, and visitors carry pathogens on boots, clothing, hands, and equipment. A single visitor from an infected farm can introduce Fusarium, Avian Influenza, or Vibrio to your operation.

VISK Solution: Boot baths (200 ppm) at every entry. Hand spray (100 ppm) before entering production areas. Visitor PPE policy enforced.

Vehicles & Equipment

Trucks, tractors, equipment, and packaging materials carry soil, water, and pathogens from other farms and suppliers. Vehicle tires can spread Phytophthora, Ralstonia, and soil-borne diseases across kilometers.

VISK Solution: Vehicle tire spray/wash at farm entrance (200 ppm). Equipment sanitation (200 ppm) before entry. Delivery staging area outside production zones.

Biological Vectors

Wild birds, rodents, insects, wind-blown spores, contaminated water flowing onto the farm. Avian Influenza via wild birds, whitefly spreading plant viruses, Phytophthora via drainage water from neighboring farms.

VISK Solution: Perimeter fogging (50 ppm) during high-risk seasons. Drainage water treatment (5 ppm at entry points). Net house barriers + VISK = layered defense.

Application Protocol

VISK Biosecurity Protocols by Farm Area

1

Entry Points

When: Daily bath changes · Continuous dispensers

  • Install foot baths at every building entry.
  • Fill with 200 ppm VISK. Change solution daily or when visibly dirty.
  • Install hand sanitizer dispensers with 100 ppm VISK solution.
  • Enforce visitor PPE policy — coveralls, boot covers, gloves.

Dilution: 200 ppm foot baths · 100 ppm hand spray

2

Vehicle & Delivery Zone

When: Every vehicle entry

  • Spray vehicle tires and undercarriage with 200 ppm at farm gate.
  • Designate a delivery staging area OUTSIDE production zones.
  • All deliveries unpacked and sanitized before moving to production.
  • Key Rule: No outside vehicle enters production areas.

Dilution: 200 ppm tire spray · Staging area outside production

3

Quarantine for New Plants/Animals

When: Every new introduction

  • Hold all incoming plants, seedlings, fish, or birds in a quarantine area for 7–14 days.
  • Treat with VISK upon arrival (50 ppm foliar spray for plants, appropriate dose for animals).
  • Monitor for symptoms before integrating into main production.
  • Key Rule: Nothing enters production without quarantine clearance.

Dilution: 50 ppm treatment · 7–14 day quarantine

4

Outbreak Response

When: As needed — emergency protocol

  • Immediately isolate affected area.
  • Increase VISK application rates to outbreak levels.
  • Restrict all movement between affected and unaffected areas.
  • All personnel entering affected area use dedicated PPE.
  • Sanitize everything leaving the area with 200 ppm.

Dilution: 200 ppm sanitation · Full isolation protocol
