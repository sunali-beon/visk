Common Threats

Why Capsicum Needs Extra Sanitation

Capsicum is a high-value crop grown in controlled environments — but those same conditions create perfect breeding grounds for devastating diseases.

Phytophthora Blight

Phytophthora capsici

Sudden wilting, stem cankers, fruit rot. The most devastating capsicum disease — can destroy an entire polyhouse crop in days.

VISK Solution: 100 ppm soil drench at planting. 50 ppm via drip weekly. Spray lower canopy with 50 ppm.

Bacterial Wilt

Ralstonia solanacearum

Rapid, complete wilting without yellowing. Plants collapse and die within days. Bacteria survive in soil for years.

VISK Solution: 50 ppm via drip irrigation continuously. 200 ppm soil drench between crop cycles.

Powdery Mildew

Leveillula taurica

Yellow patches on upper leaf surface, white powdery growth underneath. Causes defoliation and sunscald on exposed fruit.

VISK Solution: 50 ppm foliar spray targeting leaf undersides every 7 days.

Anthracnose / Fruit Rot

Colletotrichum spp.

Sunken, water-soaked lesions on ripening fruit. Orange spore masses in humid conditions. Major post-harvest loss factor.

VISK Solution: 50 ppm foliar spray during fruiting. 30 ppm post-harvest wash.

Application Protocol

Stage-by-Stage VISK Protocol for Capsicum

1

Seed Treatment

When: Before sowing

  • Soak seeds in 100 ppm VISK for 30 minutes to eliminate seed-borne bacteria and fungi.
  • Key Risk: Seed-borne bacterial wilt, Fusarium

Dilution: 100 ppm · 20 mL/100L · Soak 30 min

2

Nursery / Seedling

When: 0–35 days

  • Mist seedlings with 50 ppm every 3 days. Sanitize pro-trays with 200 ppm before reuse.
  • Key Risk: Damping off (Pythium), Phytophthora early infection

Dilution: 50 ppm · 10 mL/100L · Mist spray

3

Transplant

When: Day 30–40

  • Root dip in 50 ppm. Drench planting hole with 100 ppm VISK solution.
  • Key Risk: Phytophthora root infection, transplant shock

Dilution: 50–100 ppm · 10–20 mL/100L · Dip + drench

4

Vegetative Growth

When: Day 35–65

  • 50 ppm foliar spray weekly. 2–5 ppm continuous via drip. Focus on lower canopy and stem base.
  • Key Risk: Bacterial wilt, Phytophthora stem canker

Dilution: 50 ppm foliar · 2–5 ppm drip

5

Flowering

When: Day 55–80

  • 50 ppm fine mist every 7 days. Good air circulation + VISK prevents Botrytis on flowers.
  • Key Risk: Botrytis flower blight, powdery mildew onset

Dilution: 50 ppm · 10 mL/100L · Fine mist

6

Fruiting

When: Day 70–150

  • 50 ppm foliar spray every 7 days. Cover developing fruit for anthracnose prevention.
  • Key Risk: Anthracnose, Phytophthora fruit rot

Dilution: 50 ppm · 10 mL/100L · Full canopy spray

7

Pre-Harvest

When: 3–5 days before picking

  • 30 ppm final spray. Zero residue ensures export-safe produce.
  • Key Risk: Surface contamination, fruit rot initiation

Dilution: 30 ppm · 6 mL/100L · Light spray

8

Post-Harvest

When: Immediately after picking

  • Dip harvested peppers in 30–50 ppm VISK for 2 minutes. Air dry before packing.
  • Key Risk: E. coli, anthracnose during storage, shelf life

Dilution: 30–50 ppm · 6–10 mL/100L · Dip 2 min
