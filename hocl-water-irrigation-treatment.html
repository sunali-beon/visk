Hidden Threats

3 Hidden Threats in Your Water

Even 'clean-looking' water carries invisible threats that damage your irrigation system and infect your crops.

Biofilm

Slimy microbial colonies that coat pipe interiors, drippers, and filters. Grows in ALL water systems. Clogs drippers (up to 30% flow reduction within weeks), reduces fertigation accuracy, harbors pathogens protected from treatment.

VISK Solution: 2–5 ppm continuous eliminates biofilm over 2–3 weeks. Prevents reformation.

Waterborne Pathogens

Pythium, Phytophthora, Fusarium, E. coli, Salmonella and other microorganisms present in surface water, well water, and recycled water. Direct infection of roots and foliage. E. coli contamination of leafy greens causes foodborne illness outbreaks.

VISK Solution: 2–5 ppm in irrigation water achieves 99.999% pathogen elimination. Zero residue on crops.

Iron Bacteria

Bacteria that oxidize dissolved iron, creating rusty-orange deposits that clog entire irrigation systems. Orange sludge blocks filters, drippers, and valves. Iron staining on greenhouse plastic reduces light transmission.

VISK Solution: 50 ppm flush through affected lines weekly. 5 ppm continuous in high-iron water sources.

Treatment Protocols

Protocols by Water Source

1

Well Water

When: Continuous + monthly flush

  • Add 2–5 ppm at header tank or injection point.
  • For high-iron wells: pre-treat with 10 ppm shock, then 5 ppm continuous.
  • Flush system with 100 ppm monthly.
  • Monitor ORP: target 650–700 mV for full pathogen control.
  • Key Risk: Iron bacteria, mineral scaling, Pythium/Phytophthora from shallow wells

Dilution: 2–5 ppm continuous · 10 ppm iron · 100 ppm flush

2

Canal / Surface Water

When: At intake point

  • Pre-filter to remove sediment (VISK works best in filtered water).
  • Add 5–10 ppm at intake point (higher dose needed due to organic load).
  • Settle water for 30 min before distributing to drip.
  • Monitor ORP: may need higher dose if organic load is heavy.
  • Key Risk: Highest pathogen load — E. coli, Salmonella, Pythium, algae, organic matter

Dilution: 5–10 ppm at intake · Pre-filtration required

3

Municipal Water

When: At storage tank

  • VISK replaces municipal chlorine — no DBP formation.
  • Add 2–3 ppm to storage tank.
  • Already relatively clean — lower dose sufficient.
  • Addresses biofilm in building/farm supply pipes.
  • Key Risk: Residual chlorine (forms harmful DBPs), inconsistent quality, biofilm in supply pipes

Dilution: 2–3 ppm · Replaces chlorine

4

Recycled / Recirculated Water

When: At return tank

  • Add 2–5 ppm at return tank before recirculation.
  • System flush with 100 ppm between crop cycles.
  • Compatible with UV and ozone — complementary, not conflicting.
  • Monitor dissolved oxygen alongside ORP.
  • Key Risk: Pathogen accumulation, nutrient imbalance, Pythium and Fusarium buildup

Dilution: 2–5 ppm continuous · 100 ppm between cycles
