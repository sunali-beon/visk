About VISK

Farm Hygiene,
Backed by Science.

VISK is a food-grade Hypochlorous Acid (HOCl) concentrate — a farm sanitation input designed for modern Indian agriculture.

What We Do

Clean Water. Clean Surfaces. Safer Produce.

VISK helps farmers maintain hygienic conditions across every stage of the farm — from irrigation water treatment and greenhouse sanitation to post-harvest produce washing and equipment cleaning.

Our active ingredient, Hypochlorous Acid (HOCl), is the same molecule your immune system produces to fight pathogens. It eliminates 99.99%+ of bacteria, fungi, and viruses on contact — without leaving chemical residues, without altering taste, and without harming plants, soil, or water systems.

99.99% Pathogen elimination on surfaces and in water

pH 5–6.5 Skin-safe, plant-safe, food-grade range

Why HOCl?

The Science Behind the Solution.

Nature's Own Sanitizer

HOCl is produced by white blood cells (neutrophils) as the body's first line of defence against infection. VISK replicates this molecule through a controlled electrolysis process — the same chemistry, at farm scale.

80x More Effective

At the same concentration, HOCl is up to 80 times more effective than sodium hypochlorite (bleach). This means you use less product, at lower concentrations, with better results — and no toxic residue.

No Resistance Buildup

Unlike chemical disinfectants, microorganisms cannot develop resistance to HOCl. Its oxidative mechanism of action disrupts cell walls on contact — a physical process that pathogens cannot adapt to.

Biodegradable

After its work is done, HOCl reverts to plain saltwater. No harmful by-products, no environmental accumulation, no waiting period before harvest. It's safe for organic and conventional farming alike.
